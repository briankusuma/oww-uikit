Design Tokens

Design tokens are the foundational visual atoms of the OWW design system. They store design decisions—typography, color palettes, sizing, spacing, and border radii—in a maintainable format registered with !default flags in SCSS.

Typography (Inter)

Primary font family is Inter with a tight letter spacing (-0.02em). Scales range from 12px (text-xs) up to 48px (text-5xl) across four font weights.

Text 5xl (48px / Bold) $font-size-5xl · 48px · Line-height: 1.3
Text 4xl (36px / Bold) $font-size-4xl · 36px · Line-height: 1.3
Text 3xl (30px / Semibold) $font-size-3xl · 30px · Line-height: 1.3
Text 2xl (24px / Semibold) $font-size-2xl · 24px · Line-height: 1.2
Text xl (20px / Medium) $font-size-xl · 20px · Line-height: 1.2
Text lg (18px / Regular) $font-size-lg · 18px · Line-height: 1.5
Text md (16px / Base Body) $font-size-md · 16px · Line-height: 1.5
Text sm (14px / Medium Label) $font-size-sm · 14px · Line-height: 1.5
Text xs (12px / Caption) $font-size-xs · 12px · Line-height: 1.5
